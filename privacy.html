PRIVACY NOTE

Privacy note

Last updated 11 November 2026

This is the advance-reader site for the novel Source of Truth. It is run by one author, not a company. This note explains what the site collects, why, and how to stop hearing from me. It is written in plain language and is not legal advice.

What I collect

Only what you type into the sign-up form:

  • Your email address (required), so I can reach you.
  • Your first name (optional), so a message can greet you by name.

I collect nothing else. There is no account to create, and I do not ask for anything beyond these two fields.

Why I collect it

To send occasional news about Source of Truth and the next book, and to invite early readers to the advance copy. That is about one email a month, and usually fewer.

How the sign-up is handled

When you submit the form, it is processed by Formspree, a form-handling service, which forwards your details to my email inbox. Your details pass through Formspree's systems in transit, so their handling is governed by the Formspree privacy policy. I keep your email in my own records only to send you the messages described above.

Fonts and third parties

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches those fonts, Google may receive your IP address and basic request information, as described in the Google privacy policy. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard request data to serve the page.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. It does not store anything on your device or follow you across other sites.

How to unsubscribe

To stop receiving emails, reply to any message I send and say so, or write to me at sovren@bmaccorp.com, and I will remove you. Every email I send will also include a way to opt out.

Contact

Questions about this note, or about the details I hold on you, can go to sovren@bmaccorp.com.

Changes

I may update this note as the project moves toward publication. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

This page is for an unreleased book and is intended for invited early readers. It is not indexed by search engines. Nothing here is legal advice.
